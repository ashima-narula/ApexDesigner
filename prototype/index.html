Whose phone is this?

Open this app on three phones, choose a different role on each, then run the GP → Patient → Pharmacist demo.

I am the GP
Issue a prescription to a patient.
I am the Patient
Receive scripts, take doses, share with pharmacy.
I am the Pharmacist
Scan a patient’s script, dispense.

Prototype for UQ DECO3800/7380 — MedFile (Semester 1 2026). No data leaves the phone; all state in localStorage.
